Privacy Policy for Tero

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tero (the "App", Android package com.terogames.tero) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is published by TerO Games ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The App shows optional rewarded ads and offers in-app purchases. These work through Google services, which process some device data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only: level, high score, shards, boosters, unlocked skins, daily-reward streak, settings and a record of completed purchases. We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. If Android's backup is enabled on your device, it may be included in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your account settings, not by us.

The App uses the following Google services. They may collect and process information under their own privacy policies:

3. Advertising

All ads in the App are optional: an ad plays only when you tap a button to earn a reward (extra boosters, a revive, doubled rewards). The App has no banner ads and no ads that interrupt gameplay.

Ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID. If you are in a region that requires consent, the App asks before showing personalized ads. You can change that choice at any time from Privacy → Ad privacy choices on the App's main menu. On any device you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service. We never see or store your card or payment details. Refunds are handled through Google Play.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To deliver the rewards you choose to earn by watching ads, and to unlock items you buy.
  • To show leaderboards, if you choose to sign in to Play Games.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect information to build profiles about you ourselves.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our own systems. Local game data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings. Data held by Google services is retained under Google's policies. You can manage it at myaccount.google.com. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your personal information ourselves, most requests are fulfilled through the controls above or through Google. We will still help with any request you send us.

9. Security

Game data stays in the App's private storage on your device. All communication with Google services uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains new features. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
